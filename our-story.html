OUR STORY

From a small New York party shop to celebrations around the world.

MUCHIN has always believed that every celebration deserves to feel special.

THE BEGINNING

A simple idea: make every celebration more special.

MUCHIN began in 1995 as a small party shop in New York, built around a simple belief: every celebration deserves to feel special.

Over the years, that little shop grew into eight stores, serving families and party lovers with products for birthdays, holidays, gatherings and all the moments in between.

A NEW CHAPTER

From local stores to a global online brand.

In 2020, MUCHIN entered a new chapter by joining forces with Chinese manufacturing partners. We closed our physical stores and moved online, combining our understanding of party culture with professional product development and manufacturing.

Today, MUCHIN brings thoughtfully designed party supplies directly to customers around the world. More than 1 million customers have already chosen MUCHIN to be part of their celebrations.
